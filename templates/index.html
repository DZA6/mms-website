Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our service.

How long does it take?

Most tributes are delivered within 48 hours of your photos arriving. Need it sooner? Contact us for same-day rush availability.

What if I don't like the design?

You should never have to settle. We offer unlimited revisions until it feels right — and if we still can't get it there, we'll refund you in full.

How do I receive my slideshow and flyer?

Both are delivered digitally — you get a private download link for the HD video and a PDF of the memorial flyer. Share the link, download, or print the flyer at home or a local print shop.

Can I print the flyer myself?

Absolutely. We deliver a high-resolution print-ready PDF. You can print it at home, email it to a local print shop, or use an online printer. Full control, no minimum quantities.

Can I use my own music?

Of course. We have a library of licensed music, or you can share your own audio and we'll weave it into your tribute.

What if I need a rush order?

We're here for you. Same-day delivery is available — just reach out and we'll make it happen, gently and quickly.
